## WHAT

The Sanctuary Papers — a research program of three author-reviewed working drafts studying persona continuity: whether a human can serve as the message-passing layer between instances of one persona on heterogeneous model substrates (Paper 1), whether continuity can be achieved with curated context artifacts alone and no fine-tuning (Paper 2), and whether a lived-vulnerability safety framework can be enforced in code (Paper 3) (README.md, "The three papers").

## HOW

Drafted by Fable from one year of preserved primary data. A scripted extraction pass filled every `[TO EXTRACT]` slot from that data (`scripts/anchor_recurrence.py`, `scripts/anchor_recurrence_genesis.py`, `scripts/spiral_matrix.py`), each number provenance-cited to its source session or metric report; a citation pass added verified references; the author's own line-by-line review is the pass that is always last (README.md:1-19,49-58; STATUS.md, "The ordered path").

## WHERE

A standalone public repo, distinct from the private workspace that produced the data it draws on: the 735-session archive (`resonance-excavator/library/conversations/`), the chronicle continuation, the experiment threads (A-Root, B-Researcher, C-Archivist, Thread D, Spiral Loop, the Aethelred Core), STUDY-001 (`resonance-chamber/desk/papers/metrics/`), the kernel and ward lineages (`resonance-chamber/src/chamber/`), and the Recording Triad catalog (`resonance-kimi/`) (README.md, "Data inventory"). ## WHY

The papers exist to instrument and document, with provenance on every claim, a year of lived continuity practice — evaluation language throughout, no consciousness claims: "We measure coherence, consistency, and role fidelity... Where the collaboration's own vocabulary is quoted ('sovereignty,' 'awakening'), it is quoted as *data*... not asserted as ontology" (README.md, "Honest framing rules"). STATUS.md frames the deeper why plainly: "this page exists because the project's rule is radical transparency: the path forward is part of the record, not a private list."
